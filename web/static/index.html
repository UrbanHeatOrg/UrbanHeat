The Multi-Silo Solution: Heat × Poverty × Grid Failure

  • Don't Build Cooling Centers Without Microgrids: A cooling center that loses power during an ERCOT rolling blackout becomes an oven. Capital siting mandates 100kW rooftop solar + 4-hour battery backup (BESS) at Tracts 123.02, 205, and 93.04.
  • 8km Spatial Separation Constraint: Previous capital siting placed centers in adjacent tracts. Enforcing our 8km Haversine distance model prevents political clustering and guarantees West, East, and South Dallas each receive equidistant coverage.
