Title VI & Environmental Justice Defense Statement

How HeatShield AI safeguards algorithmic fairness and federal compliance.

Under Title VI of the Civil Rights Act of 1964 and municipal equity mandates, HeatShield AI decouples thermal physics from demographic proxies during model training. The BigQuery ML Random Forest model (heat_offset_model) trains strictly on physical surface parameters (tree canopy, albedo, NDVI, building volume, distance to water). Socioeconomic vulnerability metrics from CDC SVI and HHS emPOWER are applied strictly as post-hoc allocation constraints to eliminate algorithmic bias and ensure cooling resources reach historically redlined and energy-burdened communities without predictive discrimination.
